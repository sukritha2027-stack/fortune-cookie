Refunds & Cancellation

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The short version: Cancel any time in two taps. You keep Plus until the end of the month you've paid for. If your first Plus payment wasn't for you, ask within 7 days and we'll refund it in full.

How to cancel Fortune Cookie Plus

  • In the app: log in, go to the Plus section and tap Manage or cancel subscription. You'll be taken to the secure Dodo Payments portal, where you can cancel. You can also change your card or UPI there and download invoices.
  • By email: write to sukrithakrishna@gmail.com from your account's email address and we'll cancel it for you.

You can also revoke the AutoPay mandate in your UPI app or bank. Please cancel in the app as well so we know.

What happens when you cancel

No further payments are taken. You keep Plus until the end of your current paid month, and then your account moves to the Free plan. Your cookie calendar, fortunes and journal entries stay with you on Free.

When you can get a refund

  • Your first Plus payment: ask within 7 days of being charged and we'll refund it in full. No questions asked.
  • Charged by mistake: for duplicate charges, charges after you'd cancelled, or any other billing error, we'll refund the incorrect amount in full.
  • Monthly renewals: renewal payments aren't refundable for the month already started. Cancel any time to stop future renewals.
  • If we shut Plus down: we'll refund any unused part of your current month.

None of this limits any rights you have under Indian consumer protection law.

How refunds are paid

Email sukrithakrishna@gmail.com with the email address on your account and the date of the charge. We aim to reply within 2 working days. Approved refunds are sent through Dodo Payments to your original payment method. They usually arrive within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank. When a refund is issued, Plus ends on your account.

Questions

We're happy to help. Email sukrithakrishna@gmail.com.
