## Angle
An honest favour from a friend, plus an ask to reply with their fortune, which gives us the first real customer language. (brainstorm.md, Piece 3, angle A + C)

## Final copy

Send one by one. Every message opens with something real between you and that friend: their latest story, your last conversation, an inside joke. The ask is one thing: crack today's cookie. Posting and replying are optional extras.

**First line, pick one:**
- **A:** [name]!! [one real thing, e.g. "that Goa story was unreal"]. Also, small favour 🥠
- **B:** [name], [one real thing]. I finally launched the thing I've been building 🥠

**Body:**

It's called Fortune Cookie: you drag a few silly sliders about your day, crack a cookie, and get a fortune that's a little too specific. Free, one cookie a day.

Could you crack yours today? Bonus points if you post the card on your story with a link sticker to fortunecookie.fit ✦

https://www.fortunecookie.fit/?ref=dm-friends-day1

P.S. tell me if yours was too accurate ♡

## Founder steps
1. Crack your own cookie in the app and save the Story card (Classic style). (1 min)
2. Send the message to 15 friends one by one, writing a real first line for each; attach your card to the first few. (11 min)
3. Reply to anyone who answers within the block; repost any card they tag you in. (3 min)
