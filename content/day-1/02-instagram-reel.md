## Angle
POV: you tell the cookie you've had zero water today, and the fortune's P.S. calls you a raisin. Shows the personalisation in 14 seconds and gets sent to the friend who forgets to drink water. (brainstorm.md, Piece 2, angle A + the crack sound from angle B)

## Final copy

**On-screen hook (0–3 s), pick one:**
- **A:** `POV: you've had` / `zero water today`
- **B:** `told the cookie` / `I'm a raisin`

**Caption, pick a first line:**
- **A:** my fortune cookie called me a raisin 💧

Answer 11 silly sliders, crack your cookie, get a fortune that's a little too specific. One free cookie, fresh every day 🥠

## Visual / video

Screen-record on your phone with sound on. Use the **Classic** card style so viewers see what free users get.

| Time | Shot | On-screen text | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | The water question already open. Thumb drags the slider all the way to "I am a raisin". | Hook A or B (two lines) | App audio |
| 2–3 s | Quick taps through the remaining sliders (speed this part up to 4x in the editor so the crack lands by 3 s) | `answering honestly` / `for once` | App audio |
| 3–6 s | Tap the cookie. It cracks. | `crack ✦` | The app's crack sound (the audio hook; keep it loud) |
| 6–11 s | Paper strip unrolls: fortune, then the P.S. "drink some water, little raisin. 💧" Hold on the P.S. for 2 seconds. | `the cookie` / `called me a raisin` | Paper unroll sound |
| 11–14 s | Tap "GET MY CARD ♡", pick Story. Card fills the screen with "crack yours ✦ www.fortunecookie.fit" at the bottom. | `crack yours` / `link in bio ✦` | App audio, fade out |

**If the P.S. comes up as "hydrate. Your brain is mostly water and it's thirsty." instead** (the app picks one of the two at random): crack another cookie, or keep it and change the 6–11 s text to `the cookie` / `said hydrate`, use caption first line B, and change the cover text to `my fortune cookie` / `told me to hydrate`.

**Cover frame:** the 8–9 s frame with the P.S. visible. Add cover text: `my fortune cookie` / `called me a raisin`.

**Edit notes:** captions burned in with Instagram's own text tool (bold sans, white with dark outline), max 2 lines, kept in the middle third so the Reels UI doesn't cover them. Total length about 14 s. No music track; the crack sound is the audio.

## Founder steps
1. In the app: open a fresh daily check-in, start screen recording, record the run in one take. Retake if needed. (5 min)
2. In Instagram's reel editor: trim, speed up 2–3 s to 4x, add the 5 text overlays, set the cover. (4 min)
3. Paste the caption and post. (1 min)
