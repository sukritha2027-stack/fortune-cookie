Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

The short version: We collect only what we need to run Fortune Cookie: your login, your check-ins, fortunes and journal entries, and whether you have Plus. We never sell your data, we don't run ads, and we never see your card or UPI details. You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

1. Who is responsible

Fortune Cookie (www.fortunecookie.fit) is responsible for your personal data. Contact us at sukrithakrishna@gmail.com about anything in this policy. We handle personal data in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. What we collect

WhatWhy we need it
Account details: your name, email address and password. Passwords are stored encrypted by our login provider, and we can't read them.To create your account, log you in and contact you about your account or subscription.

We do not collect or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank details. Dodo Payments collects those directly when you pay.

3. Your check-ins and journal are personal

Some check-in questions ask about things like mood, sleep, stress or water. We use these answers only to choose your fortune and show your own trends back to you. They're never shared with anyone for advertising or profiling, and they aren't medical records.

4. Who we share data with

We share data only with the services that help us run Fortune Cookie, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase: stores your account and app data, and handles logins.
  • Vercel: hosts the website and its server functions.
  • Dodo Payments: processes payments and subscriptions as our merchant of record. It receives your email, name, payment details and billing country. See Dodo Payments' own privacy policy for how it handles this.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver fonts and code libraries to your browser. They may see your IP address when the page loads.

These providers may store data on servers outside India. We never sell your personal data. We may share it only if the law requires us to.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account and app data within 30 days. Payment and invoice records may be kept by Dodo Payments for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your account and data;
  • withdraw your consent. This means deleting your account, since we need the data to run the app.

Email sukrithakrishna@gmail.com from your account's email address and we'll respond within 30 days. If you have a complaint, contact us first. You can also contact the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules mean each account can read only its own data, and only our server can change your subscription status. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

8. Age

Fortune Cookie is meant for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a significant way, we'll tell you in the app or by email. The date at the top shows the latest version.
